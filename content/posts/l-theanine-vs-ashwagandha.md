Both are popular "natural calm" supplements, but they work on very different timelines. Picking between L-theanine vs ashwagandha starts with how your stress shows up.

## Side-by-side

| | L-theanine | Ashwagandha |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Amino acid from tea | Ayurvedic root extract |
| Onset | 30–60 minutes | 6–8 weeks of daily use |
| Typical dose | 100–400 mg | 300–600 mg |
| Best for | Situational stress, focus, pre-sleep calm | Chronic stress, poor sleep |
| Drowsiness | Rare | Possible |
| Key cautions | Few | Pregnancy, thyroid disease, rare liver injury |

## When L-theanine fits

- Before a presentation, exam or flight
- Paired with coffee to take the edge off caffeine
- An hour before bed if racing thoughts keep you awake

## When ashwagandha fits

- Weeks or months of feeling stressed and wired
- Sleep disrupted by worry
- You're willing to take it daily and reassess after 8–12 weeks

## Safety

L-theanine has a clean safety record in studies. Ashwagandha is usually well tolerated but isn't suitable in pregnancy, with hyperthyroidism, autoimmune disease or hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, and rare liver injury has been reported. Read more in [can you take ashwagandha every day](/can-you-take-ashwagandha-every-day).

## The verdict

Choose **L-theanine** for fast, gentle, as-needed calm; choose **ashwagandha** for longer-term stress when other measures haven't been enough. Learn more in our [L-theanine guide](/ingredients/l-theanine) and [ashwagandha guide](/ingredients/ashwagandha). If anxiety affects daily life, please talk to a health professional.
