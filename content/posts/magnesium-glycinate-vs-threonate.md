Magnesium L-threonate has a big promise: magnesium that reaches your brain. Glycinate is the dependable everyday form. Here's how magnesium glycinate vs threonate really compares.

## Side-by-side

| | Magnesium glycinate | Magnesium L-threonate |
|---|---|---|
| Elemental magnesium | About 14% | About 7–8% |
| Typical daily serving | 1–2 capsules | 3 capsules (about 2,000 mg compound) |
| Main claim | Gentle, well absorbed | Raises brain magnesium |
| Human evidence | General magnesium research | A few recent trials, mostly industry-funded |
| Cost per day | Low to moderate | High |

## What the threonate research shows

Animal studies suggested L-threonate raises magnesium in the brain. In humans, recent randomized trials reported improvements in self-rated sleep quality and some measures of daytime functioning or cognition. These are encouraging but small, short and largely funded by the ingredient maker. Independent replication is limited.

## Why glycinate is the default

- More magnesium per capsule
- Cheaper per day
- Gentle on the stomach
- Fine for evening use

## Who might try threonate

People specifically interested in cognitive support who've already optimized sleep, diet and basic magnesium intake, and who accept the higher cost for early-stage evidence.

For most people, **glycinate** is the better buy. **L-threonate** is an interesting but expensive option with early evidence. Considering magnesium for sleep? Compare it with melatonin in [melatonin vs magnesium for sleep](/melatonin-vs-magnesium-for-sleep).
