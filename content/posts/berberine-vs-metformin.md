Berberine vs metformin is one of the most searched supplement-versus-drug questions. Berberine is often called "nature's metformin." It's an appealing idea, but the comparison needs care, because one is a well-tested medicine and the other is a supplement.

## Side-by-side

| | Berberine | Metformin |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Plant compound, sold as a supplement | Prescription medicine |
| Typical dose | 500 mg, 2–3 times a day | 500–2,000 mg a day, prescribed |
| Blood sugar effect | Lowered fasting glucose and HbA1c in trials | Well established |
| Long-term outcome data | Limited | Decades of data |
| Quality control | Varies by brand | Regulated drug |
| Common side effects | Constipation, diarrhea, cramping | Diarrhea, nausea, B12 deficiency over time |
| Drug interactions | Many (liver enzymes) | Fewer; contrast dye, alcohol |

## What the research shows

In a 3-month pilot study of adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, berberine (500 mg three times daily) lowered HbA1c and fasting glucose by a similar amount to metformin. The study was small. Later meta-analyses also found berberine lowers blood sugar, but trial quality and product consistency vary.

Metformin, by contrast, has been used for decades, is recommended as first-line treatment in many guidelines, and has well-understood risks.

## Why you shouldn't swap

- **Unregulated dose:** supplements may not contain what the label says.
- **Interactions:** berberine affects how the liver processes many drugs.
- **Missing evidence:** we don't know whether berberine reduces diabetes complications.

## Using them together

Both lower blood sugar, so together they raise the risk of **hypoglycemia**. If you want to try berberine while on metformin, do it with your doctor, who may adjust your doses and monitor your levels. Long-term metformin can also lower vitamin B12; see our [vitamin B12 guide](/ingredients/vitamin-b12).

## The verdict

Berberine has genuine blood-sugar-lowering effects and is reasonable to discuss with a doctor, particularly for prediabetes. Metformin remains the proven option for type 2 diabetes. Read about safety over time in [can you take berberine long-term](/can-you-take-berberine-long-term).
