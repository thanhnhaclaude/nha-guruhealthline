Lutein and zeaxanthin filter blue light and protect the macula, the center of the retina.

## How to take it

AREDS2 uses 10 mg lutein and 2 mg zeaxanthin daily. Ask your eye doctor whether it's right for you. See [vision supplements](/vision/).

## Safety and interactions

Generally safe; high intakes can harmlessly tint skin yellow.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
