Rhodiola grows in cold, mountainous regions and has long been used in Scandinavian and Russian folk medicine.

## How to take it

Take 200–400 mg of a standardized extract in the morning. Compare it in [ashwagandha vs rhodiola](/ashwagandha-vs-rhodiola/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause dizziness, dry mouth or insomnia. Avoid in pregnancy; ask a doctor if you take antidepressants or diabetes medicines.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
