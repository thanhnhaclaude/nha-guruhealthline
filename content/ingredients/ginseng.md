Asian ginseng (Panax ginseng) has been used in traditional Chinese medicine for thousands of years.

## How to take it

Follow product directions and use for limited periods. See [erectile dysfunction supplements](/erectile-dysfunction/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause insomnia, headaches and digestive problems. May affect blood sugar and blood clotting.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
