Vitamin B6 helps the body use protein and make neurotransmitters.

## How to take it

Check the total B6 in all products; many B-complex and 'energy' supplements contain far more than you need. Our [label decoder](/tools/label-decoder/) flags high doses.

## Safety and interactions

Numbness and tingling in hands and feet can occur with high doses over time. Stop and see a doctor if this happens.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
