Ginkgo comes from one of the oldest living tree species.

## How to take it

We don't recommend ginkgo for memory or tinnitus. See [brain health supplements](/brain-health/) for better-supported options.

## Safety and interactions

May increase bleeding, especially with blood thinners or before surgery. Raw seeds are poisonous.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
