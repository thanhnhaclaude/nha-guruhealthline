Garcinia is a tropical fruit whose rind contains hydroxycitric acid (HCA).

## How to take it

Not recommended. Check product claims with our [snake oil detector](/tools/snake-oil/).

## Safety and interactions

Liver injury has been reported with garcinia-containing products.
