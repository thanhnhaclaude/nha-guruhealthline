Iron carries oxygen in red blood cells. Low iron causes tiredness, shortness of breath, pale skin, hair shedding and restless legs.

## How to take it

Take iron with a source of vitamin C and at least 2 hours apart from calcium, tea, coffee and antacids. Some people tolerate alternate-day dosing better. Gentler forms such as bisglycinate cause fewer stomach problems; see [ferrous bisglycinate vs ferrous sulfate](/ferrous-bisglycinate-vs-ferrous-sulfate/).

## Safety and interactions

Constipation, nausea and dark stools are common. Iron reduces absorption of levothyroxine and some antibiotics. People with hemochromatosis must avoid iron supplements. Keep iron out of reach of children.
