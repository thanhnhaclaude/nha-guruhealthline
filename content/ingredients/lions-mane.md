Lion's mane is an edible mushroom with a shaggy, white appearance.

## How to take it

Take with or without food; see [lion's mane before or after food](/articles/lions-mane-before-or-after-food/).

## Safety and interactions

Generally well tolerated; avoid with mushroom allergy.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
