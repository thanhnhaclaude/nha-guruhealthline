Milk thistle is a flowering plant used for liver complaints since ancient times.

## How to take it

Discuss with your doctor if you have liver disease. See [liver health supplements](/liver-health-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

May cause digestive upset or allergic reactions in people allergic to ragweed and related plants.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
