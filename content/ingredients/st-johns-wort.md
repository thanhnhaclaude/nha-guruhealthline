St. John's wort is a yellow-flowered herb used for centuries for mood.

## How to take it

Only under medical guidance. See [supplements for mental health](/mental-health/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause serotonin syndrome with antidepressants, make birth control fail, and reduce the effect of many drugs. Also increases sun sensitivity.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
