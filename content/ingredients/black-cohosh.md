Black cohosh is a North American plant in the buttercup family.

## How to take it

If you try it, use it short term and tell your doctor. See [menopause supplements](/menopause-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Possible stomach upset and rare liver injury. Ask your oncologist if you've had breast cancer.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
