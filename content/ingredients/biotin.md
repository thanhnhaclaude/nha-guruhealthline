Biotin helps enzymes break down fats, carbs and protein. Most diets supply plenty.

## How to take it

If you take biotin, tell your doctor before blood tests; you may be asked to stop for a few days. For hair loss, test for iron and thyroid problems first; see [hair loss supplements](/hair-loss-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Biotin is not toxic at high doses, but lab interference can lead to missed or wrong diagnoses.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
