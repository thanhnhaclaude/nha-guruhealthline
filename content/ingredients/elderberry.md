Elderberry has been used in folk medicine for centuries and is sold as syrups, gummies and lozenges.

## How to take it

Follow the product label for short-term use. See [immune support supplements](/immune-system/).

## Safety and interactions

Never eat raw elderberries. Safety in pregnancy and breastfeeding isn't established.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
