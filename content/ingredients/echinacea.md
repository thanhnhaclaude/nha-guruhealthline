Echinacea is a North American flowering plant used traditionally for infections.

## How to take it

If you try it, start at the first sign of a cold. Better-supported options are in [immune support supplements](/immune-system/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause allergic reactions, especially in people allergic to ragweed. May interact with immunosuppressants.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
