Glucosamine is a building block of cartilage. Supplements are usually made from shellfish shells.

## How to take it

Studies used 1,500 mg a day. Stop if there's no improvement after about 12 weeks. See [supplements for joint pain](/joints/).

## Safety and interactions

Generally well tolerated. May affect warfarin. Check the source if you have a shellfish allergy.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
