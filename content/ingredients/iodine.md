The thyroid uses iodine to make hormones that control metabolism.

## How to take it

Most prenatal vitamins contain 150 mcg. Otherwise, iodized salt usually covers needs. See [thyroid supplements](/thyroid-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Excess iodine can cause hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism or thyroiditis, especially in people with existing thyroid disease.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
