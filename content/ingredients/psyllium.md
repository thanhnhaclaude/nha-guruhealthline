Psyllium husk forms a gel in the gut that softens stool, binds bile acids and slows sugar absorption.

## How to take it

Mix 3–5 g in a full glass of water and build up to 5–10 g a day. Take other medicines 2 hours apart. See [supplements for high cholesterol](/cholesterol-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Gas and bloating are common at first. Always take with enough water; people with swallowing problems or bowel narrowing should ask a doctor first.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
