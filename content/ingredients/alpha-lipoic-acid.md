Alpha-lipoic acid is made in small amounts by the body and helps convert food into energy.

## How to take it

Studies commonly used 600 mg daily. See [supplements for nerve pain](/nerve-pain/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause nausea. Monitor blood sugar if you take diabetes medicines.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
