Fenugreek seeds are used as a spice in South Asian and Middle Eastern cooking.

## How to take it

Follow product directions; studies used 500–600 mg extract. See [testosterone supplements](/testosterone-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause diarrhea and gas. May lower blood sugar. Avoid in pregnancy; allergic reactions can occur in people allergic to peanuts or chickpeas.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
