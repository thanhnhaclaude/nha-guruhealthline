Red yeast rice is made by fermenting rice with a yeast used in traditional Chinese medicine.

## How to take it

Use only with your doctor's knowledge, and never alongside a statin. See [supplements for high cholesterol](/cholesterol-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Muscle pain, liver problems and drug interactions are possible, as with statins.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
