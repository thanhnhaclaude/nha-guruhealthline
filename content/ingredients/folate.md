Folate is needed to make DNA and red blood cells. Folic acid is the synthetic form used in supplements and fortified foods.

## How to take it

Take 400 mcg daily starting at least a month before trying to conceive; a prenatal vitamin usually covers it. See [fertility supplements](/fertility-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

High folic acid intake can mask vitamin B12 deficiency. Stay under 1,000 mcg a day unless prescribed. Folic acid is prescribed alongside methotrexate in some conditions.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
