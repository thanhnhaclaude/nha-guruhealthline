Selenium is part of enzymes that protect cells and help activate thyroid hormone.

## How to take it

Studies in Hashimoto's used 100–200 mcg a day. Discuss with your doctor first. See [thyroid supplements](/thyroid-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Stay under 400 mcg a day from all sources. Signs of excess include garlic breath, brittle nails and hair loss.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
