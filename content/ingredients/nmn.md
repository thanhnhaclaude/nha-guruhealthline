NMN is a molecule your cells convert into NAD+, which declines with age.

## How to take it

If you try it, choose a third-party tested product and set realistic expectations. Compare with NR in [NAD+ vs NMN](/nad-vs-nmn/).

## Safety and interactions

Short-term side effects are mild (nausea, flushing). Avoid in pregnancy; long-term data are lacking.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
