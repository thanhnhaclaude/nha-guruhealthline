Inositol is involved in insulin signaling and is found in fruits, beans and grains.

## How to take it

Studies commonly used 2–4 g of myo-inositol daily. See [PCOS supplements](/pcos-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

High doses can cause nausea or gas. Discuss with your clinician if trying to conceive.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
