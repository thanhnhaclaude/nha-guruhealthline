Turmeric is a yellow spice used in cooking and traditional medicine; curcumin is its main active compound.

## How to take it

Choose an absorption-enhanced curcumin product and take it with food. See [supplements for joint pain](/joints/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause stomach upset. May increase bleeding risk with blood thinners. Stop if you notice signs of liver problems.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
