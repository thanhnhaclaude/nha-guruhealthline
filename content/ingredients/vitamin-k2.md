Vitamin K comes as K1 (leafy greens) and K2 (fermented foods such as natto, some cheeses).

## How to take it

Typical doses are 90–200 mcg of MK-7 a day, taken with a meal containing fat. Read [can you take vitamin D and K2 together](/can-you-take-vitamin-d-and-k2-together/).

## Safety and interactions

Don't take vitamin K supplements with warfarin unless your prescriber agrees; keep intake consistent.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
