Green tea contains catechins such as EGCG and a little caffeine.

## How to take it

We don't recommend concentrated extracts for weight loss. See the honest [weight loss supplements guide](/weight-loss/).

## Safety and interactions

Stop and see a doctor if you develop yellow skin, dark urine or abdominal pain.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
