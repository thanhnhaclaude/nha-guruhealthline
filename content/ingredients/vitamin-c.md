Vitamin C is needed to make collagen, absorb iron and protect cells from damage.

## How to take it

Food first: a single orange or half a red pepper covers most of the RDA. If you supplement, 100–500 mg is plenty. Details are in [how much vitamin C per day](/how-much-vitamin-c-per-day/).

## Safety and interactions

High doses can cause diarrhea and may raise kidney stone risk in some people. People with hemochromatosis should avoid high doses.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
