Collagen is the main structural protein in skin, tendons and bone. Supplements are broken down into amino acids and small peptides.

## How to take it

Mix 2.5–10 g of powder into a drink daily for at least 8–12 weeks. Compare sources in [marine vs bovine collagen](/marine-collagen-vs-bovine-collagen/).

## Safety and interactions

Generally well tolerated. Choose third-party tested products to limit contaminants.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
