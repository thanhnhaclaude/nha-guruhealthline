Valerian root has been used for sleep and nervousness since ancient Greece and Rome.

## How to take it

Studies typically used 300–600 mg before bed for several weeks. See [valerian root vs melatonin](/valerian-root-vs-melatonin/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause headache, dizziness and morning drowsiness. Don't combine with alcohol or sedatives. Rare liver problems have been reported.
