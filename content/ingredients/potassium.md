Potassium balances sodium and is essential for nerve, muscle and heart function.

## How to take it

Prefer food sources. Use potassium supplements or salt substitutes only if your doctor agrees. See [blood pressure supplements](/blood-pressure-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

High potassium can cause dangerous heart rhythms. Check interactions with our [drug interaction lookup](/tools/supplement-drug-interactions/).

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
