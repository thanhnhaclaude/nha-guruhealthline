Isoflavones are plant compounds in soybeans with weak estrogen-like effects.

## How to take it

Studies used 40–80 mg daily for several weeks. See [menopause supplements](/menopause-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Mild stomach upset is possible. Long-term high-dose supplement safety is less clear.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
