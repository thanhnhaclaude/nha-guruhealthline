Berberine is a yellow compound from plants such as goldenseal and barberry.

## How to take it

Take 500 mg with two or three main meals. Full details are in [berberine dosage and timing](/berberine-dosage-timing/).

## Safety and interactions

Digestive side effects are common. Risk of low blood sugar with diabetes medicines. See [can you take berberine long-term](/can-you-take-berberine-long-term/).

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
