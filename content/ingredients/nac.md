N-acetylcysteine is made from the amino acid cysteine.

## How to take it

Studies used 600–1,200 mg daily. Talk to your doctor if you have a lung condition. See [lung health supplements](/lung-health/).

## Safety and interactions

Can cause nausea and digestive upset. People with asthma should use caution.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
