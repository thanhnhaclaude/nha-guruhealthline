Saw palmetto is a palm native to the southeastern United States.

## How to take it

Not recommended for BPH. See [prostate supplements](/prostate/) for options and what to ask your doctor.

## Safety and interactions

Generally well tolerated; may cause mild digestive upset or headache.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
