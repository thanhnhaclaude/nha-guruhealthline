About 99% of your body's calcium is in bones and teeth. Your body takes it from bone when intake is low.

## How to take it

Calcium carbonate is cheapest and best taken with food; calcium citrate absorbs well with or without food. Count calcium from food first, then supplement only the gap. See [bone health supplements](/bone-health-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

High intakes can cause constipation and may raise kidney stone risk. Calcium blocks absorption of iron, levothyroxine and some antibiotics, so space them apart.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
