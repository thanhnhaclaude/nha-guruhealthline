Your brain releases melatonin in darkness to signal night-time.

## How to take it

Start with 0.5–1 mg about an hour before your target bedtime. For jet lag, take it at the destination's bedtime. Compare options in [melatonin vs magnesium](/melatonin-vs-magnesium-for-sleep/).

## Safety and interactions

Side effects include morning grogginess, headache and vivid dreams. Ask a doctor before giving it to children or using it during pregnancy.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
