Menthol, the main compound in peppermint oil, relaxes smooth muscle in the intestine.

## How to take it

Take enteric-coated capsules 30–60 minutes before meals. See [gut health supplements](/gut-health/).

## Safety and interactions

May worsen heartburn or reflux. Don't apply peppermint oil to the faces of infants and young children.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
