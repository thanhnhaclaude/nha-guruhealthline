Garlic has been used as food and medicine for thousands of years.

## How to take it

Aged garlic extract is a common form. See [blood pressure supplements](/blood-pressure-supplements/).

## Safety and interactions

Causes breath and body odor, heartburn, and may increase bleeding. Garlic is toxic to dogs.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
