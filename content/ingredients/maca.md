Maca grows in the high Andes and has been eaten as a food for centuries.

## How to take it

Studies used 1.5–3 g of maca powder daily for several weeks. See [libido supplements](/libido/).

## Safety and interactions

Generally safe as a food; long-term supplement safety is less studied.

Always tell your doctor and pharmacist about the supplements you take. Check combinations with our [interaction checker](/tools/supplement-interaction-checker/).
