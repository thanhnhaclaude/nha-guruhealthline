## What protects teeth and gums

- Brush twice a day with **fluoride toothpaste**
- Clean between teeth daily
- Limit sugary snacks and drinks, especially between meals
- Don't smoke; it's a major risk factor for gum disease
- See a dentist regularly

## Warning about xylitol

Xylitol is safe for people but **dangerous for dogs**, even in small amounts. See our guide to [supplements for dogs](/supplements-for-dogs/).
