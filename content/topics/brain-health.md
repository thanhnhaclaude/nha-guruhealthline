## What matters more than any pill

- **Sleep:** memories are consolidated during sleep.
- **Exercise:** regular aerobic activity is linked to better memory and brain health.
- **Blood pressure and blood sugar:** what's good for your heart is good for your brain.
- **Hearing and social contact:** both linked to cognitive health in older age.

For practical steps, read [how to improve memory and focus naturally](/articles/how-to-improve-memory-and-focus-naturally/).

## When to see a doctor

New forgetfulness, confusion, or trouble with daily tasks should be checked. Treatable causes include B12 deficiency, thyroid problems, depression, sleep apnea and medication side effects.
