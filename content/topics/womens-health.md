## By life stage

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| Teens and 20s | Iron if periods are heavy; calcium from food |
| Planning pregnancy | Folic acid 400 mcg; review all supplements with a clinician |
| Pregnancy | Prenatal vitamin, iron as advised, DHA |
| Perimenopause and menopause | See [menopause supplements](/menopause-supplements/) |
| 50+ | Vitamin D, calcium, B12; see [supplements after 50](/supplements-after-50/) |
