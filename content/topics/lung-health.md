## What protects your lungs

- **Don't smoke or vape**; quitting at any age helps
- Get recommended **vaccines** (flu, COVID-19, pneumococcal)
- Stay active; **pulmonary rehabilitation** helps people with COPD
- Reduce exposure to indoor and outdoor air pollution

Read [how to support lung health naturally](/articles/how-to-support-lung-health-naturally/). Shortness of breath, a persistent cough or coughing blood need medical evaluation.
