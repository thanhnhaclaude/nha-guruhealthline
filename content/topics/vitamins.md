Vitamin D for people with little sun, B12 for vegans and adults over 50, folic acid before pregnancy, and iron when a test shows you're low have the clearest benefits. The US Preventive Services Task Force found insufficient evidence that multivitamins prevent heart disease or cancer, and recommends against beta-carotene and vitamin E for that purpose."
