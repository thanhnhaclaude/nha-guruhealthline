## See a professional first

Tinnitus can come from noise exposure, age-related hearing loss, earwax, ear infections, medications and, rarely, other conditions. Tinnitus in one ear, pulsing with your heartbeat, or with sudden hearing loss needs prompt medical attention.

## What helps

- **Hearing aids** when hearing loss is present
- **Sound therapy** and masking devices
- **Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)** to reduce distress
- **Protecting your ears** from loud noise
