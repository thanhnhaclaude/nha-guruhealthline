## What beats supplements

- A consistent wake-up time, even on weekends
- Morning daylight and a dim, cool bedroom at night
- No caffeine after early afternoon and limited alcohol
- **CBT-I**, the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia

More tips are in [how to sleep better naturally](/articles/how-to-sleep-better-naturally/).

## When to see a doctor

Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, very sleepy days, or insomnia lasting more than three months should be checked.
