## Protect your kidneys

- Keep blood pressure and blood sugar in target
- Avoid regular high-dose NSAID painkillers unless advised
- Stay hydrated, don't smoke
- Get kidney tests if you have diabetes, high blood pressure or a family history

Bring every supplement to your appointments. Check combinations in our [safety database](/supplement-safety-database/).
