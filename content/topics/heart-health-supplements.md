answer: "The best supplements for heart health improve specific risk factors. Psyllium fiber and plant sterols lower LDL cholesterol, omega-3s lower high triglycerides, and CoQ10 shows promise as an add-on in heart failure. Multivitamins, vitamin D and vitamin E haven't prevented heart attacks in large trials. Statins and blood pressure medicines remain the proven tools."

faq:
  - q: "Can supplements replace statins?"
    a: "No. Statins reduce heart attacks and deaths in large trials; supplements haven't shown that. Red yeast rice contains a statin-like compound and shouldn't be combined with statins."
  - q: "Is fish oil good for the heart?"
    a: "It lowers triglycerides. For people without heart disease, benefits in large trials were mixed, and high doses slightly raise the risk of atrial fibrillation."

## Heart health basics

Know your numbers (blood pressure, LDL, blood sugar), stay active, don't smoke, and follow your doctor's treatment plan. Related guides: [blood pressure](/blood-pressure-supplements/) and [cholesterol](/cholesterol-supplements/).
