answer: "The best supplements for high cholesterol are plant sterols or stanols and psyllium fiber, which each lower LDL cholesterol by a modest, reliable amount. Red yeast rice lowers LDL because it can contain the same compound as the statin lovastatin, with the same risks. Fish oil lowers triglycerides but not LDL. Supplements don't match statins for preventing heart attacks."

## Diet changes with the biggest effect

Replace saturated fats with unsaturated fats (olive oil, nuts, fish), eat more soluble fiber (oats, beans), and stay active. See also [heart health supplements](/heart-health-supplements/) and the [drug interaction lookup](/tools/supplement-drug-interactions/) for statin interactions.
