## Warning signs

Stop any supplement and see a doctor if you develop yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, pale stools or unusual fatigue. Many supplement-related liver injuries are documented in NIH's LiverTox database; our [safety database](/supplement-safety-database/) lists key warnings.
