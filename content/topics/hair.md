## Test before you buy

Ask your doctor about **ferritin**, **vitamin D**, **thyroid** tests and a **complete blood count** if you notice increased shedding. Treatments with good evidence for pattern hair loss, such as minoxidil, work better than supplements.

For more detail, read our guide to [supplements for hair loss](/hair-loss-supplements/). Taking biotin? Tell your doctor before blood tests; our [label decoder](/tools/label-decoder/) flags it.
