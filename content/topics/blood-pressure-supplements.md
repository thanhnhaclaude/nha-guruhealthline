## Danger: potassium pills with some medicines

ACE inhibitors, ARBs and spironolactone raise potassium. Adding supplements can cause dangerously high potassium. Check with our [drug interaction lookup](/tools/supplement-drug-interactions/). For the bigger picture, see [heart health supplements](/heart-health-supplements/).
