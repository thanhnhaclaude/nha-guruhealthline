## Timeline

| Time | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | Possible digestive side effects; start low |
| Weeks 2–4 | Fasting glucose may begin to fall |
| Months 2–3 | HbA1c changes measurable |

## Who should avoid it

Pregnancy and breastfeeding, newborns, and anyone on interacting medicines without medical advice. See [berberine vs metformin](/berberine-vs-metformin/) and [long-term safety](/can-you-take-berberine-long-term/).
