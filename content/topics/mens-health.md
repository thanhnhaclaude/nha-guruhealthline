## By life stage

- **20s–30s:** creatine if training, vitamin D if indoors a lot.
- **40s:** add attention to blood pressure, cholesterol and weight. See [supplements for men over 40](/supplements-for-men-over-40/).
- **50+:** B12 absorption falls; see [supplements after 50](/supplements-after-50/).
