## Start with the basics

Lifestyle changes cut the risk of prediabetes progressing to diabetes more than any supplement. See [10 natural ways to lower blood sugar](/articles/natural-ways-to-lower-blood-sugar/) for practical steps, and check your risk with our [diabetes risk score](/tools/blood-sugar-risk-score/).

## Safety

- Watch for **low blood sugar** if you take insulin or sulfonylureas: shakiness, sweating, confusion.
- **Berberine** isn't safe in pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Tell your care team about every supplement before HbA1c checks or medication changes.
