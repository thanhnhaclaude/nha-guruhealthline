## What actually drives weight loss

Weight loss comes from eating fewer calories than you burn over time. The changes with the biggest effect are:

- **More protein and fiber**, which keep you full
- **Fewer ultra-processed foods and sugary drinks**
- **Regular activity**, including strength training to protect muscle
- **7–9 hours of sleep**, since short sleep increases hunger
- **Medical treatment** when appropriate: GLP-1 medicines produced around 15% average weight loss in trials. See [berberine vs Ozempic](/berberine-vs-ozempic/) for how supplements compare.

## Red flags on weight loss products

The FDA regularly finds weight loss supplements containing hidden prescription drugs or banned stimulants. Be wary of claims like "melt fat," "no diet or exercise," or specific pounds lost. Paste any label into our [snake oil detector](/tools/snake-oil/) to check.

## Who should be careful

Avoid stimulant-based products if you have heart disease, high blood pressure, anxiety or are pregnant. Check interactions before combining supplements with medication.
