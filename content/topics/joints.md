## What helps joints most

- **Exercise and strength training:** the most effective long-term treatment for knee and hip osteoarthritis
- **Weight loss:** each kilogram lost reduces load on the knees
- **Physical therapy and proven pain relievers**, as advised by your doctor

Runners have specific needs; see [joint supplements for runners](/best-joint-supplements-for-runners/). For inflammation more broadly, see [supplements for inflammation](/inflammation-supplements/).
