answer: "The best supplements for inflammation start with omega-3s, which modestly reduce symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis. Curcumin from turmeric shows anti-inflammatory effects in lab studies and some small trials, but human results are inconsistent and absorption is poor. 'Inflammation' is broad; supplements won't replace treatment for inflammatory diseases."

## Lifestyle has the bigger effect

- A Mediterranean-style diet rich in vegetables, fish, olive oil and whole grains
- Regular physical activity
- Healthy weight, adequate sleep and not smoking
