## Symptoms and what helps

| Symptom | Strongest options |
|---|---|
| Hot flashes | Hormone therapy; non-hormonal prescriptions |
| Sleep problems | Sleep routine, CBT-I, cooling the bedroom |
| Bone loss | Calcium, vitamin D, strength training |
| Weight gain | Protein, strength training; see [women's weight loss](/womens-weight-loss/) |

Talk to your doctor about the full range of treatments; many women are good candidates for hormone therapy.
