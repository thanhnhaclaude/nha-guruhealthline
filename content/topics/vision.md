## Who should consider AREDS2

Only people diagnosed with **intermediate AMD** or **advanced AMD in one eye**. The doses are high, particularly zinc, so it isn't meant as a general eye vitamin. Ask your eye doctor.

## Protecting your eyes

- Regular eye exams, especially after 40
- Sunglasses that block UV
- Not smoking, which raises AMD and cataract risk
- Managing blood sugar and blood pressure
