answer: "The best supplements for thyroid health are mostly about avoiding harm. Iodine is essential, but extra iodine or kelp can trigger thyroid problems in people who already get enough. Selenium lowered thyroid antibodies in Hashimoto's in some meta-analyses, without clear effects on symptoms. Biotin can make thyroid blood tests misleading, and calcium and iron block thyroid medication."

## Interactions to watch

| Supplement | Issue with thyroid treatment |
|---|---|
| Calcium, iron, magnesium | Block levothyroxine absorption; separate by 4 hours |
| Biotin | Can distort thyroid blood tests |
| Ashwagandha | May raise thyroid hormone levels |
| Kelp, iodine | Can trigger hyper- or hypothyroidism |
