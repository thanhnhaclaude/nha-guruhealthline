## Food first

A diet with 25–35 g of fiber from vegetables, fruit, beans and whole grains supports a diverse gut microbiome. Fermented foods such as yogurt and kefir add live cultures.

## Choosing a probiotic

Pick a product naming the exact strain, matched to your problem. See [S. boulardii vs Lactobacillus](/saccharomyces-boulardii-vs-lactobacillus/) and [probiotics for bloating](/best-probiotics-for-bloating/).

## Red flags

Blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, anemia or symptoms that wake you at night need medical attention, not supplements.
