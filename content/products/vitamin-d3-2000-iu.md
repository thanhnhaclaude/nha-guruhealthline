## What it is

Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) at 2,000 IU per softgel, in an oil base for absorption. Vitamin D3 helps the body absorb calcium and supports normal immune function. Many people get little sun in winter, which is when supplements are most often used.

## How to use it

Take 1 softgel a day with a meal that contains some fat. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [vitamin d3 guide](/ingredients/vitamin-d3/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Do not combine with other high-dose vitamin D products without asking your doctor. Talk to your doctor first if you have kidney disease, sarcoidosis or high calcium levels. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
