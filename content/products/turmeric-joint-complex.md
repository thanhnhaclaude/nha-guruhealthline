## What it is

Turmeric root with 95% curcuminoid extract, plus glucosamine, ginger, chondroitin, boswellia and black pepper extract. Curcumin is poorly absorbed on its own; black pepper extract (BioPerine®) is added to help. Our turmeric guide explains what the joint studies used.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [turmeric guide](/ingredients/turmeric/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Contains shellfish. Talk to your doctor first if you take blood thinners or diabetes medicine, have gallbladder problems, or are pregnant. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
