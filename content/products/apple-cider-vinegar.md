## What it is

Organic apple cider vinegar powder with inulin (a prebiotic fiber), a digestive enzyme blend and a probiotic strain. Capsules avoid the acidity of liquid vinegar on teeth and throat. Evidence for weight loss is limited; our weight-loss guide explains what the trials actually found.

Talk to your doctor first if you take diabetes medicine, diuretics or digoxin, or have low potassium or acid reflux. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
