## What it is

200 mg of coenzyme Q10 as ubiquinone per capsule. CoQ10 is fat-soluble, so it is best taken with a meal that contains some fat. People taking statins often ask about it; our guide covers that research.

## How to use it

Take 1 capsule a day with a meal. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [coq10 guide](/ingredients/coq10/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Talk to your doctor first if you take warfarin, blood-pressure or diabetes medicine. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
