## What it is

Fish oil from sardines and anchovies with 300 mg of combined EPA and DHA per softgel. EPA and DHA are the omega-3 fats found in oily fish. Most people eat less fish than dietary guidelines suggest, which is why fish oil is one of the most widely used supplements.

## How to use it

Take 1 softgel a day with a meal. Take 2 a day if your doctor recommends a higher EPA + DHA intake. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [omega 3 guide](/ingredients/omega-3/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Talk to your doctor first if you take blood thinners (such as warfarin) or have a fish allergy. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
