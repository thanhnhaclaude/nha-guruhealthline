## What it is

Unflavored creatine monohydrate, 5 g per scoop. Monohydrate is the form used in most creatine research, and 3–5 g a day is the dose most studies use.

## How to use it

Mix 1 scoop into water, juice or a shake once a day, at any time of day. Drink plenty of water. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [creatine guide](/ingredients/creatine/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Talk to your doctor first if you have kidney disease or take medicines that affect the kidneys. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
