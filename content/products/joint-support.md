## What it is

A joint formula with MSM, plant-sourced glucosamine, turmeric, white willow bark, boswellia and hyaluronic acid. The glucosamine here is vegetal (from corn), not shellfish. Our glucosamine guide explains what the joint trials found and which doses they used.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [glucosamine guide](/ingredients/glucosamine/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

White willow bark contains salicin, related to aspirin: avoid if you are allergic to aspirin, and talk to your doctor if you take blood thinners, NSAIDs or are under 18. Not for use during pregnancy. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
