## What it is

Chromium, biotin and other nutrients with a blend of bitter melon, cinnamon, gymnema, banaba, mulberry and alpha lipoic acid. This formula is for people whose blood sugar is already in the normal range. It is not a treatment for diabetes; our blood sugar guide explains what the research on each ingredient shows.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with meals. Do not exceed the stated serving. 

## Before you take it

If you have diabetes or take glucose-lowering medicine (including insulin and metformin), talk to your doctor first: several ingredients can lower blood sugar further. High-dose biotin can distort lab tests. Not for use during pregnancy. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
