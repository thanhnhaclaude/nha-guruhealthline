## How to use it

## Before you take it

For adult men. Urinary symptoms can have serious causes: see a doctor before self-treating. Saw palmetto can affect PSA test results; tell your doctor you take it. Talk to your doctor first if you take blood thinners or hormone therapy. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
