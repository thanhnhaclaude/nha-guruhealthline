## What it is

Vitamins C and E, zinc and copper at levels similar to the AREDS eye study, plus bilberry, eyebright and lutein. The zinc (80 mg) is far above the everyday upper limit of 40 mg. That level was used in the AREDS trials for people with age-related macular degeneration, under medical supervision. Our vision guide explains who it is for.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal, only if your eye doctor recommends an AREDS-style formula. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [lutein guide](/ingredients/lutein/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

High-dose zinc taken long term can cause copper deficiency and stomach upset. Talk to your eye doctor before use, especially if you smoke or take other zinc supplements. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
