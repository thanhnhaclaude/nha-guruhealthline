## What it is

A broad enzyme blend that helps break down protein, fat, lactose and the sugars in beans and vegetables. Lactase and alpha-galactosidase are the enzymes behind lactose and bean-related gas; this blend includes both.

## How to use it

Take 1 capsule with your largest meal of the day. Do not exceed the stated serving. 

## Before you take it

Bromelain and papain may increase bleeding risk with blood thinners. Avoid if you are allergic to pineapple or papaya. See a doctor about ongoing digestive symptoms. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
