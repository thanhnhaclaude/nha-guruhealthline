## What it is

A botanical blend of turmeric, beet root, dandelion leaf, artichoke, ginger, milk thistle and alfalfa. The milk thistle here is a 50 mg powder rather than a standardized silymarin extract, so this is a general botanical blend, not a high-dose milk thistle product.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [milk thistle guide](/ingredients/milk-thistle/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

If you have liver disease, see your doctor rather than self-treating. Talk to your doctor first if you take blood thinners, diabetes medicine or diuretics, or have gallbladder problems. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
