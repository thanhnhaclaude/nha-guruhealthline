## What it is

Panax ginseng extract with ginkgo leaf powder and a standardized ginkgo extract (24% flavone glycosides). The 120 mg standardized ginkgo extract is at the low end of the 120–240 mg range used in studies; our ginkgo guide covers what they found.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal, preferably earlier in the day. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [ginkgo guide](/ingredients/ginkgo/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Ginkgo and ginseng can increase bleeding risk: stop 2 weeks before surgery and talk to your doctor first if you take blood thinners, antidepressants or diabetes medicine. Not for use during pregnancy. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
