## What it is

Biotin (vitamin B7). High-dose biotin can distort some lab tests, including thyroid and heart tests, so tell your doctor you take it.

## How to use it

Follow the directions on the label and do not exceed the stated serving. Our [biotin guide](/ingredients/biotin/) explains typical doses, timing and the research behind them.

## Talk to your doctor first if you

- are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy
- take prescription medicine or have a medical condition
- are scheduled for surgery
