## What it is

A night-time blend with 2 mg melatonin, valerian, chamomile, lemon balm, passion flower, GABA and L-tryptophan. Our [melatonin guide](/ingredients/melatonin/) explains why many people do well on less (often 0.5–1 mg), so start with 1 capsule.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules 30–60 minutes before bed. Start with 1 capsule to see how you respond. Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [valerian guide](/ingredients/valerian/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Causes drowsiness: do not drive or drink alcohol after taking it. Not for children, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Talk to your doctor first if you take antidepressants (L-tryptophan), sedatives, blood thinners or blood-pressure medicine. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
