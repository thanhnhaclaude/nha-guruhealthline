## What it is

Magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine. Each 3-capsule serving gives 275 mg of elemental magnesium, about two-thirds of the Daily Value. Glycinate is the form most people choose for an evening routine because it is easier on the stomach than magnesium oxide or citrate.

## How to use it

Take 3 capsules a day with water, for example with dinner. You can split the dose (1 in the morning, 2 in the evening). Do not exceed the stated serving. Our [magnesium guide](/ingredients/magnesium/) explains typical doses, timing and the research.

## Before you take it

Talk to your doctor first if you have kidney disease or take antibiotics, bisphosphonates or blood-pressure medicine; magnesium can affect how some medicines are absorbed. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
