## What it is

A chelated form of magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine. Glycinate is the form most people pick for evening use because it is gentle on the stomach compared with magnesium oxide or citrate.

## How to use it

Follow the directions on the label and do not exceed the stated serving. Our [magnesium glycinate guide](/ingredients/magnesium/) explains typical doses, timing and the research behind them.

## Talk to your doctor first if you

- are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy
- take prescription medicine or have a medical condition
- are scheduled for surgery
