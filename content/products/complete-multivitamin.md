## What it is

A two-capsule daily multivitamin with 20 vitamins and minerals plus botanical blends. A multivitamin is a simple way to cover common gaps. It contains no iron, which suits most men and post-menopausal women; read our vitamins guide for who may need more of a specific nutrient.

## How to use it

Take 2 capsules a day with a meal. Do not exceed the stated serving. 

## Before you take it

Contains vitamin A and folic acid: talk to your doctor if you are pregnant or planning pregnancy. Contains green tea extract and saw palmetto. Talk to your doctor if you take blood thinners or other supplements with the same nutrients. Keep out of reach of children. Store in a cool, dry place.
