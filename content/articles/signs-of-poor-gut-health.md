Your digestive system usually lets you know when something is off. These twelve signs are common, and most are manageable.

## Twelve common signs

1. Frequent **bloating**
2. Excess **gas**
3. **Constipation**: fewer than three bowel movements a week or hard stools
4. **Diarrhea** or loose stools
5. Alternating constipation and diarrhea
6. **Abdominal pain** or cramping
7. **Heartburn** or acid reflux
8. **Nausea**
9. New **food intolerances**
10. Feeling full very quickly
11. **Fatigue** with digestive symptoms
12. Changes in bowel habits lasting more than a few weeks

## Red flags: see a doctor

- Blood in stool or black stools
- Unexplained weight loss
- Persistent vomiting
- Difficulty swallowing
- Anemia or fever
- Symptoms that wake you at night
- New bowel changes after age 50

## Steps that help

- Build up fiber slowly to 25–35 g a day
- Drink water, move daily and manage stress
- Eat fermented foods if you tolerate them
- Keep a food and symptom diary
