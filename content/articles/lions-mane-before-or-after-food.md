Lion's mane (*Hericium erinaceus*) is an edible mushroom sold as a "nootropic" for memory and focus. If you've bought some, here's how to take it.

## Before or after food?

- **With food:** easiest on the stomach and simple to remember.
- **Without food:** fine if it doesn't upset your stomach.
- **Morning or evening:** no evidence favors either; morning is common for focus goals.

Consistency matters more than timing.

## What the evidence shows

- A small 2009 trial in Japanese adults with mild cognitive impairment found better cognitive scores with lion's mane powder over 16 weeks, which declined after stopping.
- Later small studies report mixed effects on memory and mood.
- A 2025 systematic review concluded the evidence is promising but limited, with mostly small, short studies.

## Safety

Lion's mane appears generally well tolerated. Possible side effects include stomach upset and skin rash. Avoid it if you're allergic to mushrooms, and talk to a doctor if you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or take blood thinners or diabetes medicines.

## The bottom line

Take lion's mane whenever it fits your routine, ideally with a meal. Keep expectations modest. For better-supported options, see our [brain health guide](/brain-health/) and [evidence database](/tools/evidence-db/).
