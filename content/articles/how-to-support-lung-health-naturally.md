Your lungs filter around 10,000 liters of air a day. Protecting them is mostly about what you breathe in and how active you stay.

## Eight steps

1. **Don't smoke or vape.** Quitting is the single most important step at any age.
2. **Avoid secondhand smoke.**
3. **Stay active.** Exercise strengthens breathing muscles and improves fitness.
4. **Get vaccinated** against flu, COVID-19 and pneumococcal disease as recommended.
5. **Reduce air pollution exposure**: check air quality, avoid exercising near heavy traffic, ventilate when cooking.
6. **Test your home for radon**, a leading cause of lung cancer in non-smokers.
7. **Protect yourself at work** from dust and fumes with proper equipment.
8. **Treat asthma and allergies** with your prescribed plan.

## Warning signs

See a doctor for a cough lasting more than three weeks, coughing blood, shortness of breath with mild activity, wheezing or chest pain.

## Supplements

Vitamin D reduced COPD flare-ups in people who were deficient, and NAC may help some people with chronic bronchitis. See [supplements for lung health](/lung-health/).
