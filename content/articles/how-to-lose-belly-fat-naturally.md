Belly fat comes in two types: fat under the skin and **visceral fat** around the organs. Visceral fat is linked to type 2 diabetes and heart disease, which makes reducing it worthwhile even without big changes on the scale.

## What reduces belly fat

1. **A modest calorie deficit.** Fat loss happens across the whole body; you can't choose where it comes from.
2. **Protein at every meal**, which keeps you full and protects muscle.
3. **More fiber**, from vegetables, beans and whole grains.
4. **Fewer sugary drinks and refined carbs.**
5. **Aerobic exercise.** Regular moderate activity reduces visceral fat even without much weight loss.
6. **Strength training**, to maintain muscle and metabolic rate.
7. **7–9 hours of sleep.** Short sleep is linked to more abdominal fat.
8. **Less alcohol**, which adds calories and is linked to abdominal weight gain.
9. **Stress management**, since chronic stress can affect eating and sleep.

## Measure progress

Track your **waist circumference** as well as weight. A waist over 40 inches (102 cm) in men or 35 inches (88 cm) in women signals higher health risk.

## Supplements and belly fat
