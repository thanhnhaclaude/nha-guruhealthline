High blood sugar develops gradually, and so does improvement. These ten steps have the best evidence.

## Ten steps

1. **Walk for 10–15 minutes after meals.** Muscles pull glucose from the blood.
2. **Build meals around vegetables, protein and fiber.**
3. **Swap refined carbs for whole grains and beans.**
4. **Drop sugary drinks**, including juice and sweetened coffee.
5. **Lose 5–10% of body weight** if you're overweight.
6. **Strength train** two or three times a week to improve insulin sensitivity.
7. **Sleep 7–9 hours.** Poor sleep raises insulin resistance.
8. **Manage stress**, which raises blood sugar through stress hormones.
9. **Limit alcohol**, and don't drink on an empty stomach if you take diabetes medicines.
10. **Monitor your numbers** and work with your care team.

## Supplements that may help

Berberine and psyllium have the best evidence for lowering blood sugar, with smaller effects than lifestyle changes. See [best supplements for blood sugar](/blood-sugar/) and check your risk with the [diabetes risk score](/tools/blood-sugar-risk-score/).
