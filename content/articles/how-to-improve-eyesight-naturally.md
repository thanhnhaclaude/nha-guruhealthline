Blurry vision is usually caused by the shape of the eye or by the lens stiffening with age. These are mechanical problems, which is why they need glasses, contacts or surgery, not supplements.

## Habits that protect your eyes

1. **Get regular eye exams.** Many eye diseases, such as glaucoma, have no early symptoms.
2. **Follow the 20-20-20 rule.** Every 20 minutes of screen use, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to reduce eye strain.
3. **Wear sunglasses** that block UV rays.
4. **Don't smoke.** Smoking raises the risk of cataracts and macular degeneration.
5. **Manage blood sugar and blood pressure** to protect the retina.
6. **Eat leafy greens and fish**, which are linked to lower risk of eye disease.
7. **Use good lighting** for reading and close work.
8. **Spend time outdoors**, which is linked to lower rates of nearsightedness developing in children.

## Where supplements fit

The AREDS2 formula slows progression of **intermediate age-related macular degeneration**. It isn't for general eyesight improvement. See our [vision supplements guide](/vision/).

## When to see an eye doctor

Sudden vision loss, flashes or floaters, eye pain, or a curtain over your vision are emergencies.
