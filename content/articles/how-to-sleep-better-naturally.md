Most sleep problems improve with changes to routine and environment. These ten tips are drawn from the core of CBT-I and sleep research.

## Ten tips

1. **Wake up at the same time every day**, even on weekends.
2. **Get bright light in the morning**, ideally outdoors, to set your body clock.
3. **Dim lights in the evening** and limit screens in the last hour.
4. **Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet.**
5. **Cut off caffeine 6–8 hours before bed.**
6. **Limit alcohol**; it fragments sleep in the second half of the night.
7. **Exercise regularly**, but finish intense workouts a few hours before bed.
8. **Use the bed only for sleep and sex**, not for phones or work.
9. **Get up if you can't sleep** after about 20 minutes, and return when sleepy.
10. **Wind down** with a consistent pre-sleep routine.

## When to see a doctor

Loud snoring with breathing pauses, gasping at night, restless legs, or insomnia lasting over three months deserve medical attention.
