## Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the **United States** only, including APO/FPO addresses where the carrier allows it. We don't ship internationally yet.

## Shipping costs

| Order subtotal | Shipping |
|---|---|
| Under $50 | $5.95 flat rate |
| $50 or more | Free |

The shipping cost is shown in your cart and again at checkout before you pay.

## Delivery times

Orders are packed and shipped by our fulfillment partner from a US warehouse, usually within **2–4 business days** after payment. Transit then typically takes 2–5 business days, depending on the carrier and your location. Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees.

## Tracking

As soon as your order ships, we email you the carrier and tracking number. You can also check your order status from the link in your confirmation email.

## Problems with a delivery

If your package is marked delivered but you can't find it, check with neighbors and your local post office, then email us within 14 days at editor@guruhealthline.com with your order number. If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, see our [Returns & Refunds policy](/returns-refunds/).

## Address changes

Please double-check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us right away; we can update the address only if the order hasn't been packed yet.
