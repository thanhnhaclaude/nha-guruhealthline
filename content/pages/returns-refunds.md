## 30-day returns on unopened items

You can return **unopened, unused** products in their original packaging within **30 days** of delivery for a refund of the product price. For safety reasons we can't accept returns of opened supplements.

To start a return, email editor@guruhealthline.com with your order number and the items you want to return. We'll reply with the return address and instructions. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, and original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the return is due to our mistake.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, email us within **14 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo of the item and packaging. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you. You won't need to send the item back.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card through Stripe, or your PayPal account) within 5 business days of approval. Your bank or PayPal may take another 5–10 business days to show the credit.

## Cancelling an order

Email us as soon as possible. If the order hasn't been packed yet, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has shipped, the return terms above apply.
