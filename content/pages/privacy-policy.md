## What we collect

GuruHealthline doesn't require an account and doesn't sell personal data. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and troubleshooting.

## Affiliate links

When you click a link to a store such as Amazon or iHerb, that store may set cookies to attribute a purchase to us. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

## Analytics

If we add analytics, we'll list the provider here and offer a way to opt out.
