## Who we are

This website (guruhealthline.com) is operated by **SPARKX MARKETING CO., LIMITED**, 411 108th Avenue Northeast, Suite 700, Bellevue, WA 98004, United States ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

## What we collect

GuruHealthline doesn't require an account and doesn't sell personal data. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and troubleshooting.

## Affiliate links

When you click a link to a store such as Amazon or iHerb, that store may set cookies to attribute a purchase to us. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

## Orders from our shop

When you buy from the shop we collect your name, email address, phone number and shipping address, plus the items you ordered. We use them to process payment, ship your order, send order and shipping emails, and handle returns. We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

We share only what each partner needs:

- **Stripe** and **PayPal** process payments. You enter your card or PayPal details on their pages; we never receive your full card number.
- **Shopify** and **Supliful** receive your name, address, phone, email and items so the order can be packed and shipped.
- Our email provider (Hostinger) sends order emails.

Your cart is stored only in your own browser (local storage) until you check out.

## Analytics

If we add analytics, we'll list the provider here and offer a way to opt out.
